# Synthetic insurance documents

All documents in this folder are fictional training data created for an AI document intelligence project.
They are not real policies, legal advice, or real customer/claim records.

Files:
- policy_001.pdf: fictional comprehensive motor policy
- policy_002.pdf: fictional third-party-only motor policy
- claim_001.txt: fictional accidental-damage claim referencing policy NS-MTR-1001

Use these documents to test multi-document ingestion, page-aware PDF extraction, source metadata, retrieval, citations, and missing-document identification.
