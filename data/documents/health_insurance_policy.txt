HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY

Policy Overview:
This policy provides financial coverage for eligible medical expenses
incurred during hospitalization due to illness or accidental injury.

Hospitalization Coverage:
The policy covers hospitalization expenses for a minimum of 24 hours.
Covered expenses include room charges, nursing fees, doctor's fees,
surgery costs, and prescribed medicines.

Pre-Hospitalization:
Medical expenses incurred up to 30 days before hospitalization
are covered, provided they are related to the same medical condition.

Post-Hospitalization:
Eligible medical expenses incurred up to 60 days after discharge
are covered under the policy.

Waiting Period:
A general waiting period of 30 days applies from the policy start date.
Accidental hospitalization is covered from the policy commencement date.

Exclusions:
Cosmetic procedures, self-inflicted injuries, non-prescribed treatments,
and expenses related to excluded medical conditions are not covered.

Claim Process:
Policyholders must submit hospital bills, discharge summaries,
medical prescriptions, and valid identity documents.
Cashless claims require pre-authorization from the insurer.
