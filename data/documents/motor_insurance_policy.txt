
MOTOR INSURANCE POLICY

Policy Overview:
This policy provides financial protection against losses involving
the insured vehicle due to accidents, theft, fire, and specified
natural disasters.

Accidental Damage:
The policy covers repair or replacement costs resulting from
accidental damage to the insured vehicle, subject to policy terms
and applicable deductibles.

Third-Party Liability:
The policy covers eligible legal liabilities arising from bodily
injury, death, or property damage caused to third parties
by the insured vehicle.

Theft Coverage:
In case of vehicle theft, the policyholder must immediately report
the incident to the police and notify the insurance company.
A police FIR and required vehicle ownership documents must
be submitted for claim processing.

Deductibles:
A compulsory deductible applies to every eligible own-damage claim.
The deductible amount depends on the vehicle category and
applicable policy conditions.

Exclusions:
Damage caused while driving without a valid driving licence,
driving under the influence of alcohol, and normal wear and tear
are excluded from coverage.

Claim Process:
The policyholder must notify the insurer after an incident.
The insurer may arrange a vehicle inspection before approving repairs.
Original repair invoices and supporting documents must be submitted.
