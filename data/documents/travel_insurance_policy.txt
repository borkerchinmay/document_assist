
TRAVEL INSURANCE POLICY

Policy Overview:
This policy provides financial protection against specified
unexpected events occurring during domestic or international travel.

Medical Emergency:
Eligible emergency medical expenses incurred during travel
are covered up to the policy's specified coverage limit.

Trip Cancellation:
The policy covers eligible non-refundable travel expenses
when a trip is cancelled due to specified reasons such as
serious illness, accidental injury, or death of an immediate family member.

Baggage Loss:
Loss of checked-in baggage may be covered subject to the
policy limits and airline documentation requirements.

Travel Delay:
Eligible additional accommodation and meal expenses caused
by covered travel delays may be reimbursed according to
the policy conditions.

Exclusions:
Pre-existing medical conditions, voluntary trip cancellations,
unattended baggage losses, and losses resulting from illegal activities
are excluded unless specifically covered by an endorsement.

Claim Process:
Policyholders must notify the insurer as soon as reasonably possible.
Supporting documents may include flight tickets, boarding passes,
medical reports, airline loss reports, receipts, and proof of payment.
